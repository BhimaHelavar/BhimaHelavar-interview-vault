Questions

Who is this for?

Developers with 2 to 10 years of experience preparing for .NET and Angular roles, and graduates aiming at roles that expect about five years of knowledge.

How do I get in?

Open the unlock page, enter your key once, and the library opens on that device. The key is stored in your browser. Use the same key on another device.

Is it a PDF?

No. It is a website you read in the browser, on phone or laptop, with search, level filters and progress tracking.

Does it guarantee a job?

No. It teaches the concepts and the way to explain them. Interviews also depend on your practice and experience.

Can I share my key?

The key is for you only. Shared keys can be disabled.
