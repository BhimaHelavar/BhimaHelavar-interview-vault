How every lesson is written

The same seven steps every time, so you always know where to look.

  1. The problemA small story that shows why the topic exists.
  2. The ideaA plain analogy before any jargon.
  3. How it worksStep by step, with a diagram.
  4. ImplementationShort, realistic code.
  5. PitfallsWhat goes wrong in production.
  6. At workWhere you will meet it in enterprise apps.
  7. Interview questionsAnswers plus a one-line "say this" summary.

Every question is tagged Junior, Mid or Senior, has a search box, and a checkbox to track what you can already explain aloud (progress stays in your browser).
